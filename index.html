Mission

Advancing nature services through geospatial AI

We integrate field ecology with multi-sensor remote sensing and machine learning to monitor forest carbon, biodiversity, climate extremes, and ecological tipping points — and to support JREDD+ and NetZero MRV.

Approach

Multi-scale monitoring

One pipeline from ground truth to Earth observation — so models stay tied to ecological reality.

  1. Field plots — forest carbon inventory, traits, and biodiversity
  2. UAV / drone — fine-scale canopy and structure
  3. Terrestrial LiDAR — 3D stand architecture
  4. Airborne platforms — landscape mapping
  5. Satellites — GEDI, Sentinel-1/2, Landsat, TanDEM-X

Research pillars

Five themes, one lab

Mangroves are a flagship model system — methods transfer across urban forests, tropical forests, coasts, and agroforestry.

Forest carbon

Biomass, soil carbon, blue carbon, and urban green-space sinks.

GeoAI

Multi-sensor fusion, TensorFlow, scikit-learn, XGBoost, GEE, and SEM.

JREDD+ & MRV

Jurisdictional carbon monitoring for high-integrity crediting and NetZero.

Climate extremes

Heat, drought, salinity, and cyclone impacts on carbon and recovery.

Ecological tipping points

Critical slowing down, kNDVI resilience signals, and early warnings.

Focus

Ecosystems & applications

Hong Kong / Greater Bay Area, South and Southeast Asia, and global coastal landscapes — with Essential Biodiversity Variables woven through.

Essential Biodiversity Variables

Species, traits, community composition, and ecosystem function for policy-ready monitoring.

Urban & tropical forests

Green space carbon, tropical stand structure, and agroforestry systems.

Coastal mangroves

Flagship model for blue carbon, biodiversity co-benefits, and resilience.

Nature-based solutions

Climate adaptation, coastal protection, biodiversity conservation, and carbon trading.
