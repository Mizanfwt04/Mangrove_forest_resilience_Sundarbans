About CLEAR

Resilience starts with communities.

CLEAR is an emerging platform for adaptation in coasts, mangroves, tropical forests, agroforestry and urban greenspace. Communities lead the design and stewardship; ecological evidence helps identify what is changing and which responses support lasting recovery.

Alongside the GAINS research programme, CLEAR develops the community adaptation and restoration side of the same ecological questions.

Focus areas

From questions to practical directions.

Four connected areas guide the platform’s development and future collaborations.

01

Community-led adaptation

Local knowledge, participatory planning and stewardship models for climate-ready landscapes.

02

Ecological resilience

Forest structure, biodiversity, recovery after extremes and potential early signals of ecological change.

03

Restoration & nature-based solutions

Protect remnant ecosystems and restore the ecological structure and function that support biodiversity, carbon and livelihoods.

04

Climate extremes & livelihoods

Connect responses to cyclones, salinity, heat and compound stress with the priorities of local communities.

The approach

Evidence at every step.

A proposed pathway for developing work with partners and learning from its outcomes.

  1. ListenStart with local priorities, knowledge and experience.
  2. ObserveCombine community observations with field ecology and spatial evidence.
  3. AdaptCo-design restoration and stewardship options that can be evaluated.
  4. LearnMonitor outcomes, share findings and revise the approach together.

CLEAR is in development. Conversations are welcome with community organisations, NGOs, local institutions and researchers interested in shaping future adaptation work.
