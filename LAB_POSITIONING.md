# GAINS — positioning

**Full name:** Global Artificial Intelligence and Nature Solutions  
**Short:** GAINS · thegains.org

## One-sentence identity

GAINS is an international research institute advancing **AI-powered nature solutions** — integrating ecological theory, GeoAI, remote sensing, forest carbon, biodiversity, and climate resilience for Net Zero and nature-based solutions.

## What we are building toward

- Forest Carbon & Climate Change (MRV, JREDD+, sequestration, stability)
- Climate Extremes, Resilience & Tipping Points (CSD, kNDVI, compound extremes, early warning)
- Biodiversity & Ecosystem Functioning (functional composition, SEM, multifunctionality)
- AI for Earth (ML/DL, GeoAI, foundation models, XAI)
- Remote Sensing (satellite, drone, LiDAR, GEDI, hyperspectral, radar)
- Digital Twin Forests

## Institute posture

Not a personal CV page and not a generic AI shop. Theory first; AI and Earth observation scale mechanism for governments, universities, NGOs, and industry.
