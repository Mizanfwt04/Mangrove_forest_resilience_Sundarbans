About TRACE

Environmental claims should be traceable to evidence

TRACE is an emerging GAINS platform for measurement, reporting, and verification. It connects field protocols, remote sensing, accounting rules, uncertainty, and transparent documentation so environmental performance can be assessed without overstating what the evidence shows.

Mission

Build credible and reproducible systems for measuring, accounting, reporting, and independently checking environmental outcomes.

Vision

Decisions and investments grounded in transparent data, explicit uncertainty, ecological integrity, and accountable methods.

Principles

Traceable evidence. Conservative claims. Additionality. Permanence. Transparent uncertainty. No automatic equivalence between metrics.

GAINS connection

GAINS and its platforms generate scientific and operational data; TRACE structures the evidence needed for reporting, assurance, and finance-readiness.

Pillars

Four focus areas

TRACE is being developed around the following capabilities. These are program directions, not claims of certification or registry authority.

  1. 01

    Spatial MRV

    Field, satellite, UAV, and LiDAR evidence integrated through transparent workflows for monitoring, reporting, and verification.

  2. 02

    Carbon & nature accounting

    Baselines, inventories, additionality, leakage, permanence, biodiversity indicators, and uncertainty reported with clear boundaries.

  3. 03

    Supply-chain & ESG evidence

    Location-aware environmental footprints and documented evidence that support responsible reporting and risk screening.

  4. 04

    Assurance & finance readiness

    Methods, data rooms, audit trails, and decision support for projects seeking external review, investment, or recognized standards.

Approach

How TRACE works

A transparent chain from project definition to evidence-based reporting.

  • DefineSet boundaries, baselines, indicators, standards, and decision rules before measurement
  • MeasureCombine field observations and Earth observation with documented quality controls
  • VerifyTest consistency, uncertainty, additionality, and potential sources of bias
  • ReportProduce traceable evidence packages without converting estimates into unsupported claims
