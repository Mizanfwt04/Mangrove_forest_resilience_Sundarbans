About CLEAR

Resilience starts with communities

CLEAR — Community Lead Ecological Adaptation & Resilience — is an emerging platform focused on adaptation and resilience where people live and work: coasts, mangroves, tropical forests, agroforestry, and urban greenspace. Science informs decisions; communities lead design and stewardship.

Mission

Strengthen ecological adaptation and resilience through community-led practice, evidence-based ecology, and nature-based solutions under intensifying climate extremes.

Vision

Landscapes where local stewards, scientists, and partners co-design restoration and adaptation pathways that protect biodiversity, carbon, and livelihoods.

Values

Community leadership. Ecological integrity. Honest evidence. Adaptation that serves both people and nature.

Sister platform

CLEAR works alongside GAINS (Global Artificial Intelligence and Nature Solutions) — CLEAR centers community adaptation; GAINS centers GeoAI, theory, and monitoring science.

Pillars

Four focus areas

CLEAR organizes work around adaptation and resilience — not as open programs yet, but as the agenda we are building toward.

  1. 01

    Community-led adaptation

    Local knowledge, participatory planning, and stewardship models for climate-ready landscapes.

  2. 02

    Ecological resilience

    Structural diversity, recovery after extremes, and early signals of tipping risk in forests and coasts.

  3. 03

    Nature-based solutions

    Restoration and aquasilviculture that rebuild structure and ecological function — not canopy cover alone.

  4. 04

    Climate extremes & livelihoods

    Cyclones, salinity, heat, and compound stress — with pathways that protect ecosystems and community well-being.

Approach

How CLEAR works

A simple loop: listen with communities, measure what matters ecologically, adapt together, and share what works.

  • Listen Community priorities, local knowledge, and lived climate risk
  • Measure Structure, biodiversity, carbon, and recovery after extremes
  • Adapt Co-designed restoration, aquasilviculture, and stewardship actions
  • Share Practical lessons for partners, agencies, and peer communities
