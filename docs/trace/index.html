About TRACE

Every environmental claim needs an evidence trail.

TRACE is an emerging platform for measurement, reporting and verification. It connects field protocols, remote sensing, accounting boundaries and uncertainty so environmental outcomes can be assessed through reproducible methods and transparent documentation.

GAINS provides ecological and spatial research. TRACE develops the methods and documentation that connect those observations to reporting, external review and decision-making.

This direction connects carbon and biodiversity baselines, forest-change monitoring and uncertainty analysis with jurisdictional REDD+ (JREDD+), evidence for Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs), and carbon-neutrality pathways. Transparent records help communities, institutions and decision-makers assess environmental outcomes and their implications for human well-being.

Focus areas

From questions to practical directions.

Four connected areas guide the platform’s development and future collaborations.

01

Spatial monitoring & MRV

Integrate field, satellite, drone and LiDAR observations through transparent monitoring, reporting and verification workflows.

02

Carbon & nature accounting

Define baselines and inventories, report uncertainty, and evaluate biodiversity indicators, permanence, additionality and leakage where relevant.

03

Supply-chain & ESG evidence

Build location-aware environmental assessments and documented evidence for responsible reporting and risk screening.

04

Assurance & finance readiness

Develop data records, audit trails and decision support that help projects prepare for independent external review.

The approach

Evidence at every step.

A proposed pathway for developing work with partners and learning from its outcomes.

  1. DefineSpecify the question, project boundary, indicators and baseline.
  2. MeasureCombine field and spatial observations with documented methods.
  3. AccountQuantify outcomes and uncertainty with reproducible workflows.
  4. ReportMake the evidence, limitations and review trail accessible.

TRACE is in development. It does not issue carbon credits, certify projects or replace accredited validation and verification bodies.
