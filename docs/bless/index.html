About BLESS

Build useful systems within ecological limits.

BLESS is an emerging platform for responsible bioeconomy and circularity. It connects resource assessment, engineering and implementation research, with attention to life-cycle impacts, community needs and the conditions required for a viable field pilot.

GAINS contributes the wider ecological and GeoAI framework. BLESS focuses on how that evidence can inform resource use, technology development and practical implementation.

Focus areas

From questions to practical directions.

Four connected areas guide the platform’s development and future collaborations.

01

Biomass & low-carbon energy

Assess biological feedstocks, conversion pathways and energy systems across their full life cycle.

02

Bioprocesses & microalgae

Explore bioprocess optimisation, aquatic biomass and responsible pathways for resource recovery.

03

Circular materials

Investigate wood, agricultural residues, recovered polymers and bio-based composites for durable, lower-waste applications.

04

Pollution & sustainable infrastructure

Connect spatial evidence with pollution prevention, recovery and reuse strategies for urban and regional systems.

The approach

Evidence at every step.

A proposed pathway for developing work with partners and learning from its outcomes.

  1. AssessCharacterise resources, needs and ecological constraints.
  2. CompareEvaluate technology options and life-cycle trade-offs.
  3. TestDevelop feasible pilot designs with appropriate technical partners.
  4. EvaluateMeasure performance and impacts before considering scale.

BLESS is in development. Its focus areas describe a research and implementation agenda; pilot activities depend on suitable partners, technical feasibility and funding.
