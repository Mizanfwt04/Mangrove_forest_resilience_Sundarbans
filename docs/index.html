What we do

Intelligence for landscapes under pressure

GAINS builds geospatial models that detect change, quantify risk, and guide restoration where nature services are most vulnerable.

  • 01

    Sense

    Map vegetation, water, heat, and disturbance from satellite and field data.

  • 02

    Learn

    Train spatial AI to find resilience patterns humans miss at landscape scale.

  • 03

    Serve

    Deliver clear maps and metrics for conservation, policy, and community action.

Focus

Where nature services meet spatial AI

From mangrove coasts to humid forests, we prioritize places where climate stress and ecosystem function collide.

Coastal resilience

Disturbance recovery, cyclone impact, and blue-carbon landscapes.

Forest stability

Canopy health, carbon dynamics, and long-term vegetation trajectories.

Climate stress

Humid-heat compounds and cascading risks across living systems.
