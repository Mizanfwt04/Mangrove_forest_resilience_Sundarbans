What we do

Intelligence for landscapes under pressure

The Gains Lab builds spatial models that detect change, quantify resilience, and guide nature-based solutions where ecosystems are most vulnerable.

  • 01

    Sense

    Map vegetation, structure, and disturbance with satellite, UAV, and LiDAR observations.

  • 02

    Learn

    Use GeoAI and trait-based models to reveal drivers of carbon storage and recovery.

  • 03

    Serve

    Deliver EBVs, resilience metrics, and maps for conservation, JREDD+, and NetZero action.

Focus

Where we work

From the Sundarbans to Hong Kong coasts, we prioritize places where climate stress and ecosystem services collide.

Essential Biodiversity Variables

Species populations, traits, community composition, and ecosystem function — monitored so biodiversity informs restoration and policy.

Blue carbon & NetZero

Mangrove carbon stocks, sequestration rates, and nature-based mitigation.

Resilience science

Perturbation recovery, trait-based drivers, and landscape early-warning signals.

GeoAI monitoring

Multi-sensor fusion — Sentinel, Landsat, GEDI, TanDEM-X, UAV/LiDAR, and field ecology.
