# The Gains Lab — positioning

**Full name:** Geospatial Artificial Intelligence in Nature Services  
**Short:** The Gains · thegains.org

## One-sentence identity

We scale **ecological theory** — how biodiversity and functional composition sustain **multiple ecosystem properties and processes** — using **structural equation modeling (SEM)** and **GeoAI**.

## Scientific core (from the papers)

1. **Biodiversity–ecosystem functioning (BEF)**  
   Co-benefits of biodiversity protection and carbon storage (*Nature Communications*, 2021).

2. **Functional composition > richness alone**  
   Functional composition outweighs taxonomic and functional diversity in maintaining ecosystem properties and processes (*Global Change Biology*, 2024).

3. **Resilience, structural diversity, and tipping points**  
   Functional composition (e.g. MCH, SLA) and structural diversity enhance resilience; SEM links biodiversity, climate, disturbance, and recovery; CSD / kNDVI for early warnings (*Communications Earth & Environment*, 2026).

4. **SEM as the analytical spine**  
   Direct and indirect pathways among traits, diversity, environment, perturbation, and outcomes — not black-box prediction alone.

5. **GeoAI as the scaling layer**  
   Field → UAV/TLS → satellite fusion to test theory at landscape to jurisdictional scales (JREDD+ / MRV / NetZero).

## What we are not

Not a generic “AI for environment” shop. AI and remote sensing **serve** ecological mechanism and theory.

## Ecosystems

Mangroves as flagship model; methods transfer to urban forests, tropical forests, and agroforestry.

## Methods stack

- Structural equation modeling (SEM)  
- Essential Biodiversity Variables (EBVs)  
- Trait-based ecology  
- Multi-sensor GeoAI / GEE / ML  
- Long-term resilience & critical slowing down
